# AI Reflection Compass

## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence can help humans solve problems quickly and efficiently. However, AI systems may sometimes generate incorrect or misleading information, known as AI hallucinations.

This project, “AI Reflection Compass,” demonstrates a structured process where humans use AI responsibly through verification, critical thinking, and reflection.

The goal of this project is to combine AI assistance with human decision-making to ensure accurate and ethical outcomes.

---

## Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- To understand responsible AI usage
- To reduce AI hallucinations through verification
- To improve human decision-making
- To create a structured reflection process
- To encourage ethical AI collaboration
